# Unit 10: Itemized Bill Splitter UI & Debt Settlement Hub — GalaPH

This specification details the frontend user interface and interactive components for the Philippine KKB (Kanya-Kanyang Bayad) itemized bill splitter, avatar tagging, non-drinker exclusion mechanics, proportional service charge apportionment, greedy debt graph solver visualization, and GCash/Maya settlement payloads.

---

## 1. Scope & Deliverables

1. **Itemized Bill Splitter & Expense Logger Modal (`<CreateExpenseModal />`)**:
   - Line-item dish and item builder (Name, Unit Price ₱, Quantity, Category).
   - Category selection (`FOOD_AND_DINING`, `ALCOHOL_AND_BAR`, `TOLL_HIGHWAY`, `FUEL_AND_GAS`, `ACCOMMODATION`, `ACTIVITIES`, `GROCERIES`, `OTHER`).
   - Per-dish member avatar tagging: Touch/click to assign eaters/consumers per dish.
   - Smart barkada preset splits:
     - "All Barkada" (everyone shares).
     - "Drinkers Only" (auto-excludes `isNonDrinker: true`).
     - "Carpool Passengers Only" (auto-exempts `isDriver: true`).
   - Proportional Service Charge (SC %) and local tax/VAT apportionment math matching the backend `LedgerService`.
   - Real-time live split summary showing per-person breakdown before submitting.

2. **KKB Ledger Dashboard (`apps/web/src/app/ledger/page.tsx` & `<TripLedgerView />`)**:
   - Spend Summary Bento: Total Trip Spent, Highest Category, Average Per Person, Personal Net Balance.
   - Filterable expense card feed with itemized line items, category badges, payer avatars, and individual share breakdowns.
   - Interactive deletion / settlement status tags.

3. **Debt Graph Simplification & Settlement Hub (`<DebtSettlementCard />`)**:
   - Visual Net Balance list for all trip participants ($\sum \text{NetBalance} \equiv 0$).
   - Collapsed bilateral debt settlement cards generated by the greedy debt solver algorithm ($O(N^2) \to \le N-1$).
   - Philippine E-Wallet Payloads: Display GCash and Maya phone numbers with 1-tap copy, QR payment mockup, and "Mark as Settled" confirmation.

4. **Web Client Integration**:
   - Standalone `/ledger` route with trip context.
   - Embedded tab in `/trips/[id]` for direct trip ledger management.
   - Comprehensive demo seed data covering a classic La Union / Tagaytay barkada bill (Dampa seafood dinner, Flotsam bar tab with non-drinker protection, TPLEX gas & toll split).

5. **Quality Gates**:
   - 100% Prettier formatting compliance.
   - Zero TypeScript (`tsc --noEmit`) and ESLint warnings.
   - Next.js static production build (`npm run build:web`).
   - 76/76 passing automated backend tests.

---

## 2. Technical Invariants & Rules

1. **Centavo Arithmetic & Currency Precision**:
   - All monetary calculations must use integer centavos via `@gala-ph/shared` helpers (`formatPHP`, `pesosToCentavos`, `centavosToPesos`).
   - Rounding remainders must be apportioned deterministically to ensure $\sum \text{Splits} \equiv \text{Total Amount}$.

2. **Non-Drinker & Driver Invariants**:
   - Members flagged with `isNonDrinker: true` must default to untagged on `ALCOHOL_AND_BAR` items.
   - Carpool toll/gas splits offer 1-click driver exemption as Philippine road trip courtesy.

3. **Anti-Slop Visual Standards**:
   - Adhere strictly to Philippine travel design tokens in `ui-context.md`.
   - Creditor balances must use Nature Emerald (`#059669`), Debtor balances use Sunset Coral (`#E11D48`/`#F97316`).
